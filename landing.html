From sensor to verdict

Every number goes through the same three steps, and every tool's "How it's measured" panel shows the formula behind it.

Sense

  • Accelerometer and gyroscope, about 60 readings a second
  • Microphone, 48,000 samples a second
  • GPS position and speed, once a second
  • Touch timing for reactions

Compute

  • Gravity tracking to find "down", whichever way the phone lies
  • RMS windows, Fourier transforms, Schroeder decay curves
  • Integration with drift correction, zero-crossing timing

Judge

  • Against published limits: ISO 2631-1, ANSI S12.60, NIOSH, CIBSE Guide D
  • Against your own baseline or a known-good part
  • Verdicts always carry words and an icon, never colour alone

Phone mics and accelerometers aren't calibrated instruments. Noise has a calibration step, and the rest are best for comparing like with like.

About 60 motion readings a second means vibration is visible up to about 30 Hz.

GPS is good to about 5–10 m, so bump pins can sit a few metres off.

Not a medical or certified safety device.
